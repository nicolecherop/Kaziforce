Academic project prototype

Privacy information

Registration stores your name, email address, selected account role and a password hash in the configured project database. Session and CSRF cookies support sign-in and protect submitted forms.

Test data

Use fictional personal details during local development. Do not reuse a password from another service. Candidate profiles store the professional details you choose to add, including skills, education, certifications, experience and portfolio links.

Documents and applications

CVs, degrees and certificates are stored in a private project directory as original attachments. Profile information is entered by you. Uploading a document does not change your profile.

Each application stores a separate copy of the details you choose to submit. Only you can see a draft. When you submit, the recruiter who owns that job can access the submitted profile and selected document copies. Later edits to your profile or removal of a library document do not change previously submitted applications.

You can remove library documents from CV & documents. Submitted application copies remain for recruitment records. Self-service deletion of submitted applications is not currently available. Arrange removal with the project owner. Administrators with access to the project server or database can access stored information, and project backups may retain earlier copies until their retention period ends.

Access and deletion

Project administrators can manage test accounts through Django administration. Self-service account deletion and data retention procedures have not yet been implemented. Arrange deletion with the project owner when finishing a test.

Before collecting real information

This page documents the current prototype. A reviewed privacy notice, retention schedule and deletion process are required before collecting real candidate data.
