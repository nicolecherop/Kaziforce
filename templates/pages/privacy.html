Your information and choices

Privacy Policy

Your account stores your name, email address, selected account role and a password hash. Cookies support sign-in and help protect submitted forms.

Profile information

Your professional profile contains the details you choose to add, including skills, education, certifications, experience and portfolio links. These details support job matching and can be used as a starting point for your applications.

Documents and applications

You may add a contact email and phone number to your profile and choose the contact details shared with each application. Recruiters can use the submitted details to contact you. Contact information does not contribute to matching scores.

Interview invitations

A recruiter may share an interview time, joining details and their contact information through your application. Updates and cancellations appear there too. When email delivery is connected, interview messages are sent to the contact email submitted with that application, or its saved account email if no separate contact email was provided. Email delivery records are kept with the interview. Further arrangements can continue directly by email or phone.

CVs, degrees and certificates are stored privately as original attachments. Profile information is entered by you. Uploading a document does not change your profile.

Each application stores a separate copy of the details you choose to submit. Only you can see a draft. When you submit, the recruiter who owns that job can access the submitted profile and selected document copies. Later edits to your profile or removal of a library document do not change previously submitted applications.

You can remove library documents from CV & documents. Submitted application copies remain for recruitment records. To request removal of an application, contact the KaziForce administrator. Authorized administrators can access stored information, and backups may retain earlier copies.

Freelance services and bookings

Freelance listings are optional. When you enable your listing and agree to share your professional information, signed-in recruiters can see your services, skills, experience, education and certifications. Portfolio links become available to a recruiter after an accepted booking with you. Uploaded documents and profile contact fields are not included in the public listing. Turning off new bookings hides your listing; existing conversations and booking records remain available to their participants.

Messages, booking terms and payment reports are visible to the participants and authorised administrators. Both parties' saved contact details are shared within the booking only after the freelancer accepts and agrees to disclose their details. These saved booking contacts are independent of later profile changes. Reviews from completed bookings appear with the freelance listing unless hidden by an administrator. Reports about a conversation are available to authorised administrators and are not shown to the other participant.

Freelance text is checked for obvious contact details and web links to limit sharing before booking. A rejected enquiry is not sent. Existing text may be masked when displayed to someone without an accepted booking; this does not rewrite the original record. Portfolio links are hidden until an accepted booking exists with that recruiter. Text checks cannot detect every deliberate disguise.

Access and deletion

To request account or data removal, contact the KaziForce administrator. Removing a library document does not remove copies previously submitted with an application.

Account security

Use a strong, unique password and keep your sign-in details private. Sign out when using a shared device.
