How can we help?

Getting started

Create an account and choose Candidate if you are looking for opportunities, or Recruiter if you are looking for talent. Use your email address to sign in.

Account access

Forgotten your password? Request a reset link using the email address associated with your account.

Your professional profile

Fill in your profile manually with skills, education, certifications, work experience and portfolio links. Upload your CV and supporting qualification documents through CV & documents. CVs accept PDF/DOCX; degree documents and certificates accept PDF/JPG/PNG, up to 5 MB each. Scanned documents are accepted.

Applying for a job

Open Find jobs, choose a role and select Start application. Your saved profile fills the application form. Keep it as it is or tailor the details for that job. Save a private draft or choose documents and submit. Application edits do not change your main profile. Once submitted, that copy stays with the application.

Recruiter access

Recruiters can post and close their own jobs, compare ranked candidate matches, read submitted profiles and attachments, and update application statuses. Candidates can follow updates in My applications. Drafts and unselected library files stay private.

Freelance services and short assignments

Open Freelance work in your workspace. Candidates can create a services listing and choose whether to accept new bookings. Recruiters can find freelancers, publish short projects and discuss the work in Messages. A booking becomes confirmed when the freelancer accepts its scope, dates, price and cancellation terms. Accepted bookings reserve their full date range.

Both parties' saved contact details unlock when the freelancer accepts the booking. No advance payment is required. Work payments can be recorded after completion is confirmed. Cash and direct payments are arranged between you: the sender reports payment, and the recipient confirms receipt. KaziForce does not collect or hold these payments. The worker marks work complete and the recruiter confirms it before leaving a review. Refresh Messages to see new messages and booking updates.

Before an accepted booking, keep contact details and web links out of messages, listings and projects. You can discuss the assignment, dates and price in-app. Portfolio links become available to the recruiter after acceptance. Once booked together, you can share contact information in your private conversation.
