Academic project prototype

Prototype terms

KaziForce is being developed for a university project. Features and stored test data may change during development. Use fictional profile details when testing.

Use of this prototype

Create a candidate or recruiter account to test the available account workflows. Do not upload another person's information, confidential records or misleading job postings.

Matching and hiring

Matching features are still under development. Once available, scores will describe profile and job similarity and support human review. They will not guarantee employment or make hiring decisions.

Before public use

These prototype terms require review before a public launch. Real candidate information must not be collected until retention, deletion and operational support procedures are established.
