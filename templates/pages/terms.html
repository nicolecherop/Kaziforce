Your account and responsibilities

Terms of use

KaziForce connects candidates and recruiters through professional profiles, job opportunities and skill-based matching.

Using KaziForce

Create a candidate account to explore opportunities or a recruiter account to find talent. Keep your information accurate. Do not upload another person's information without permission, confidential records or misleading job postings.

Matching and hiring

Match scores describe the alignment between a professional profile and a job's requirements. They support human review and do not guarantee employment or make hiring decisions. Recruiters remain responsible for reviewing applications and supporting documents.

Freelance assignments

Agree the scope, dates, price and cancellation or refund terms before accepting a booking. A booking is confirmed only when the freelancer accepts. Changes to a project listing do not change an existing booking agreement. Confirmed bookings require both parties to agree to cancellation. No advance payment is required. Payment is recorded after the recruiter confirms completion. Completion and payment receipt are recorded separately.

Cash and direct payments are arranged between the participants. KaziForce records reported payments and recipient confirmations; it does not collect, hold or verify these payments through a payment provider. Report only money actually sent or received. Reviews must relate to completed bookings. Ratings and recommendations support your own assessment and do not verify qualifications or guarantee work quality.

Your information

You choose the profile details and documents to share with each application. Read our Privacy Policy for information about account data, documents and application copies.
