Shared records remain: applications already submitted (including their attached document copies), listings, messages, booking agreements, reviews and payment records remain available to the other participants and authorised administrators. Future interviews are cancelled. You will be signed out on all devices. This affects both your candidate and recruiter workspaces and cannot be undone.
